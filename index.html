OBSERVED VS MODELED

No fake precision.

The tailwater gauge tells us when the river begins responding below the dam. USACE operations can confirm release-level outflow. Belva measures downstream discharge. The release-wave engine combines those signals only after onset evidence exists, then widens arrival uncertainty downstream.
